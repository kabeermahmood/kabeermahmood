### 📌 Featured Projects

#### 🤟 [Signverse](https://github.com/kabeermahmood/Signverse-2.0) — ASL Translation Platform
A full-stack web platform for real-time American Sign Language detection and learning. Uses a **Random Forest ML model** (90%+ accuracy) with MediaPipe hand landmark detection to translate live webcam feeds and uploaded images. Includes a searchable dictionary of **3000+ ASL signs**, an interactive learning hub with self-assessment, JWT authentication, and a community resources section for the deaf community.

`React` `Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB` `Python` `Flask` `MediaPipe` `scikit-learn` `TailwindCSS`

---

#### 🎬 [SceneForge](https://github.com/kabeermahmood/SceneForge) — AI Script-to-Scene Image Generator
An AI pipeline that transforms a video script into a complete set of visually consistent scene illustrations. **Google Gemini** analyzes the script to extract characters, settings, and mood into a Character Bible — then generates illustrations for every scene with consistent style across 6 art styles and 3 aspect ratios. Built for animated YouTube videos and short-form content creators.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `Google Gemini API` `Zustand` `JSZip`

---

#### 📍 [FINDIN](https://github.com/kabeermahmood/FINDIN-MAIN) — Lost & Found Platform
A React Native mobile app that connects people who have lost or found items, providing a community-driven platform for reuniting people with their belongings.

`React Native` `JavaScript`
